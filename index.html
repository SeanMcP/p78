PGH Stop Watch

A Chrome extension that counts down to the next Pittsburgh bus at your stop, right in your toolbar.

It's in development and not on the Chrome Web Store yet. To try it, follow the steps in the README.

Privacy policy

The extension doesn't collect personal data, use analytics, or show ads.

  • It saves your stop and your settings in Chrome's storage, which Chrome may sync to your other devices.
  • It downloads live bus arrivals from Pittsburgh Regional Transit's public feed. Those requests don't include anything about you.
  • It checks whether your computer is in use, so it can stop downloading arrivals while you're away. Chrome answers that on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Questions? Open an issue.
